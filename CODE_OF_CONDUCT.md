# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct — Short Form

gmail-tui adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) Code of Conduct, **version 2.1**. This file is the short form; the authoritative, complete text is at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> and applies in full to this project.

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Be welcoming and professional: use kind and inclusive language, respect differing opinions and experiences, accept constructive criticism gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without consent, and any other conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities and Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior and will take fair, appropriate corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful — including removing comments, editing or removing issues/PRs, and temporarily or permanently banning contributors.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team privately: contact the maintainers directly through GitHub (private message on the [andsigno82 profile](https://github.com/andsigno82) — not a public issue). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The project team is obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, code comments) and also applies when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.
